# Prompt 30 known limits

- Benchmarks are bounded VPS measurements, not hardware-independent SLAs.
- The public corpus is a compact legal allow-list; it is not the world's PDFs.
- External tool availability depends on VPS provisioning and is classified honestly.
- Cargo metadata is a fallback SBOM when dedicated SBOM tooling is unavailable.
- Deployment TLS, trust stores, HSM integration, and live revocation retrieval remain
  operator configuration concerns.
- Fuzzing, corpus runs, and external comparisons improve confidence; they do not prove
  every PDF is safe or standards-conformant.
